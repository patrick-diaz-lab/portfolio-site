Telegram was the first way to talk to Vision and is still the one on my phone. The adapter between the two is deliberately thin: it passes my messages to [agent core](/vision/agent-core/) and the answers back, with no model, no database and no records of its own.

It is also where Vision learns who is talking to it. Every message the adapter passes on is signed, and agent core accepts nothing else. This page names those layers and why each exists; the details stay private.
