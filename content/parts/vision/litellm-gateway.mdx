Several services in Vision talk to language models: the chat agent, the eval harness, the indexer. None of them holds a provider key or names a model. They all send their requests to one LiteLLM gateway that I host myself, and ask for a role instead of a model: `smart`, `cheap`, `local` or `embed`.

The gateway decides which concrete model sits behind each role, knows what every call costs, and stops a flow that has spent its share. That gives me one place to switch providers, one place to read the bill, and one place where a runaway loop hits a wall.
