Several services in Vision talk to language models: the chat agent, the eval harness, the indexer. None of them holds a provider key or names a model. They all send their requests to one LiteLLM gateway that I host myself, and ask for an alias instead of a model: `smart` (the strong model), `cheap` (the cheap one), `local` (a small model on my GPU PC) or `embed` (the embedding model).

The gateway decides which concrete model sits behind each alias, knows what every call costs, and stops a flow that has spent its share. That gives me one place to switch providers, one place to read the bill, and one place where a runaway loop hits a wall.
