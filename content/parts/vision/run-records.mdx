Every service in Vision that does work writes the same kind of record: the chat agent, the indexer, the [MCP servers](/vision/mcp-servers/), the eval harness and the cleanup jobs. A run stands for one piece of work, and every model call, tool call or command inside it is a step with its tokens, cost and time. One query then answers what ran yesterday, what it cost and what failed, across everything.

The records hold names, counts and costs. The words themselves, the question, what each step saw and the answer, live in a separate trace store for the [console](/vision/console/), and that store forgets them after 30 days.
