### Read the terms first, then set limits

Before building anything I read the consumer terms, the usage policy, the tool's documentation on authentication and running without a screen, and the support articles, and wrote down the passages that apply. They didn't settle whether a personal assistant that passes on its owner's own requests counts as ordinary, individual use, so I took the narrow reading. The worker runs only for requests I send myself, never on a schedule or in the background. It uses the official tool with the sign-in that tool creates itself, never a copied or forwarded credential. And it stops at the first warning or unusual throttling, and then I decide how to go on.
